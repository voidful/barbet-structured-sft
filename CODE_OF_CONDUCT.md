# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds
and levels of experience. Critique technical work without personal attacks.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and deliberate spam
are not acceptable in issues, reviews or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation.
Report abuse through GitHub's reporting tools; do not repost private
details in a public issue. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions
when doing so will not expose private information.
